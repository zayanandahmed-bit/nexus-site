What we build

Four systems that give you your hours back.

Each one built to your process and connected to the tools you already pay for. Most clients start with one and add the next.

01↗

Customer agents

An assistant that handles what your front desk repeats all day — hours, pricing, availability — then books, reschedules or cancels straight into your calendar.

  • WhatsApp, web chat, SMS or email
  • Answers from your own prices and policies
  • Hands anything sensitive to a human
02↗

Lead pipelines

Systems that find businesses matching your ideal customer, enrich them with real contact details, and hand your sales team a ranked list instead of a spreadsheet of noise.

  • Sourced and de-duplicated automatically
  • Contact details verified, not guessed
  • Scored so you work the best leads first
03↗

Workflow automation

The quiet plumbing: moving data between your calendar, sheets, CRM and inbox so nobody re-types anything. Built to fail loudly rather than silently drop a booking.

  • Connects the tools you already use
  • Handles the edge cases, not just the happy path
  • Alerts you when something needs a person
04↗

Live dashboards

One screen that tells you what's actually happening — pipeline, bookings, response times — built on live data instead of a monthly export somebody assembles by hand.

  • Live data, not last month's snapshot
  • Filters and exports your team will use
  • Shareable with whoever needs it

Process

From first call to live system.

No six-month discovery phase. We find the task costing you the most hours, build that first, and put it in front of real users quickly.

  1. 01

    Map the bottleneck

    A call to find where the hours actually go. It's usually not the task you'd guess — it's the small interruptions around it.

  2. 02

    Build the first system

    We build the highest-cost workflow first, wired to your real tools and data, so you can judge it on your own cases.

  3. 03

    Test against reality

    It runs alongside your team before it runs instead of them. We watch where it gets things wrong and tighten it.

  4. 04

    Hand over & extend

    You get the system, the documentation and the keys. Then we look at the next bottleneck — or you take it from there.

FAQ

Questions worth asking.

Anything else, just ask — we'd rather you know exactly what you're getting.

How long until something is live?

For a single well-defined workflow, usually two to four weeks from the first call. Bigger systems get broken into pieces so something useful runs early rather than everything landing at the end.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many systems and how tangled the current setup is. We quote a fixed price per system after the first call, so you're never signing up to an open-ended hourly bill.

Do we have to change the tools we already use?

Usually not. Most of what we build connects to what you have — your calendar, sheets, CRM, inbox. Replacing working tools is expensive and rarely the actual problem.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

It gets designed for. Our systems hand over to a human on anything sensitive or unclear rather than guessing, and log what they did so you can see it. An agent that admits uncertainty is worth more than one that's confidently wrong.

Who owns the system once it's built?

You do — the workflows, the code and the accounts, with documentation. You're not renting access to something you can't touch.

Is our data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts wherever possible, and we use the minimum access a system needs to work. We'll walk through exactly what connects to what before anything goes live.
