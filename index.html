FAQ

Questions worth asking

How long until something is live?

For a single well-defined workflow, usually two to four weeks from the first call. Bigger systems get broken into pieces so something useful runs early rather than everything landing at the end.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many systems and how tangled the current setup is. We quote a fixed price per system after the first call, so you're never signing up to an open-ended hourly bill.

Do we have to change the tools we already use?

Usually not. Most of what we build connects to what you have — your calendar, sheets, CRM, inbox. Replacing working tools is expensive and rarely the actual problem.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

It gets designed for. Our systems hand over to a human on anything sensitive or unclear rather than guessing, and log what they did so you can see it. An agent that admits uncertainty is worth more than one that's confidently wrong.

Who owns the system once it's built?

You do — the workflows, the code and the accounts, with documentation. You're not renting access to something you can't touch.

Is our data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts wherever possible, and we use the minimum access a system needs to work. We'll walk through exactly what connects to what before anything goes live.
